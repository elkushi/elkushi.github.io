Sound familiar?

Four reasons a clinic loses patients even when its doctors are good. One part of the solution fixes each of them.

A patient couldn’t get through and booked somewhere else

Fix: online booking on the site and in the bot works around the clock. After a missed call the patient gets a message at once, and the front desk gets a reminder to call back.

Patients forget their appointment and the chair sits empty

Fix: the bot reminds them a day and two hours before. The patient confirms or moves the visit with one button, so a freed slot can go to someone else.

On a phone, prices and booking are hard to find

Fix: the new site is built for phones first. Services and prices, doctors, licence and booking are in plain sight, no zooming or searching.

Happy patients leave without a word

Fix: after the visit the bot asks for a review on Google Maps, Yandex Maps or 2GIS. Fresh reviews help new patients choose you.

Common questions

How much does the concept cost?

Nothing. The concept doesn’t commit you to anything: if you don’t like it, you simply keep it.

What if a patient doesn’t use the messenger?

They book on the site or by phone, as before. The bot is one more convenient way in; it doesn’t replace calling the clinic.

Do we need to change our clinic software?

No. Requests reach the front desk in the messenger, and they confirm each one in one click. Linking to the schedule in your clinic software is discussed separately.

What do you need from the clinic?

A list of services with prices, information about the doctors and photos if you have them. The clinic approves all texts about medical services. I do everything else.
