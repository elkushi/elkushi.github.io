What the clinic gets

A new site built for phones

Services and prices, doctors, reviews, licence details and every required document. It loads fast and reads without zooming. Your front desk updates prices and offers without calling a developer.

Online booking

The patient picks a free time on the site or in the bot. The front desk confirms it in one click. Moving or cancelling a visit takes a couple of taps, no phone calls.

A clinic bot in the messenger patients use

Telegram and MAX in Russia, WhatsApp or Telegram elsewhere. It answers common questions (address, hours, how to prepare for a visit) and sends reminders a day and two hours before the appointment. The front desk sees each new request right away.

Reviews after the visit

After the appointment the patient gets a link to leave a review on Google Maps, Yandex Maps or 2GIS. More fresh reviews mean more trust from new patients.

Common questions

How much does the concept cost?

Nothing. The concept doesn’t commit you to anything: if you don’t like it, you simply keep it.

What if a patient doesn’t use the messenger?

They book on the site or by phone, as before. The bot is one more convenient way in; it doesn’t replace calling the clinic.

Do we need to change our clinic software?

No. Requests reach the front desk in the messenger, and they confirm each one in one click. Linking to the schedule in your clinic software is discussed separately.

What do you need from the clinic?

A list of services with prices, information about the doctors and photos if you have them. The clinic approves all texts about medical services. I do everything else.
